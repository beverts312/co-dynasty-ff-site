General Rules
1. Buy In= $125. Owners who take over an existing team pay a discounted $75 for their first year only, then $125 after that (Jake and Nick in 2026).
2. Join and Be Active on [Slack Channel](https://coloradodynasty.slack.com)
3. [Sleeper App](https://sleeper.com/leagues/1312151922802688000) is the website/app to review for League Settings, Scoring, etc 
Other Rules
4. No locking rosters for Pickup/Dropoffs of teams that didn't make the playoffs.
5. Rosters will lock when End of Fantasy Football Season
6. FA/Rookie Draft will be the same draft
7. The draft will be non-snaking and reverse order of standings to help out the shitty teams in the dynasty league. Standings are ordered by: Max PF for teams that didn't make the playoffs, then playoff round, then record, then Max PF. (Max PF = the most points a team could have scored with its best possible lineup each week, as shown on Sleeper.)
8. Teams need to have an active roster each week. (No Non-Filled spots). Penalty will be losing 1 draft spot. If last place, you will get 2nd to last place.
9. Draft will be 6 rounds and include a new feature of taxi squad (3 new roster spots on taxi squad) Taxi Squad=Rookie + 1 Year (Total 2 Years)
10. 3 IR Spots Currently
11. Owners will be required to add to taxi or drop players on their roster before the start of week 1 to reach maximum roster numbers. (Active/Bench Spots/Taxi only, IR is not included)
12. Payouts (2026)=$840 1st Place, $420 2nd Place, $10 Weekly Points (highest score each week, 14 weeks, $140 total). The pot is $1,400 this year because Jake and Nick are at the discounted $75. Once everyone pays $125, the extra will likely roll into the weekly points payouts.
13. 6 Teams makes playoffs with 2 teams first round bye.
